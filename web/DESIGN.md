# Design System: OpenLC

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Money You Can Watch"**

OpenLC is one product with two rooms. The landing is a monochrome cinematic world: a black ground, silver-white type, glossy near-black cards, luminous white lines, and white pill actions, with no hue anywhere. It shows money being locked and released as light moving along a line, never as a colour. The workspace is a dark operations dashboard, pinned by the owner from a reference: cool near-black greys, a collapsible sidebar, a blurred sticky header, and one green accent that only ever means "active" or "done".

Both rooms share Mona Sans, a flat near-white headline treatment, and the same refusal of decoration: size, weight and position carry hierarchy, and colour is reserved for state. The landing performs (a wave-line tunnel, a centre-staff timeline that lights as you scroll, a bar that physically splits); the workspace does not. Its only entry motion is the Overview tile stagger, and it puts money state and the next action first.

Density differs by room. The landing is spacious, centred and set in large type. The workspace is a 14px productivity scale in a 1320px column, where amounts are right-aligned, hashes are monospace, and every row leads to one action.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Two worlds, one type family: Mona Sans everywhere, Bricolage Grotesque only for landing display headlines.
- Landing: white is the only colour; emphasis is light (glow, gradient-lit silver), not hue.
- Workspace: cool greys tinted to hue 260; green for active and success, amber for waiting, red for claims and problems.
- Flat near-white headlines, no gradient text, no eyebrow labels.
- Tonal layering in the workspace; shadows only on things that float.

## Colors

The landing uses black and white with nothing between them but greys. The workspace uses cool near-black greys with three state hues, and each hue has exactly one meaning.

### Primary
- **Ledger Green** (ops-green): the workspace's single accent. It marks the active sidebar item (icon plus a 4px indicator bar), the focus ring, checked checkboxes and radios, completed stepper steps and connector lines, the "connected" status dot, and success pills and notices. It never decorates.

### Secondary
- **Waiting Amber** (waiting-amber): anything waiting on someone, such as "confirmation required", "inspection due", "settlement pending", a wrong-network pill, and extraction warnings.

### Tertiary
- **Claim Red** (claim-red): claims, disputed money, errors and destructive actions. The disputed release segment, the flagged stepper step, the invalid input border and the danger pill all use it.

All three state hues also appear as a *soft* fill mixed at 14% into transparent (`color-mix(in oklch, <hue> 14%, transparent)`), always paired with the full-strength hue as text or border.

### Neutral
- **Operations Black** (ops-ground): the workspace page ground.
- **Sidebar Black** (ops-sidebar): the fixed sidebar, one step lighter than the ground.
- **Panel Black** (ops-card): cards, panels, tiles, dialogs and popovers.
- **Raised Grey** (ops-raised): inputs, segmented controls, neutral pills, the active nav row and hover fills (often mixed at 55%).
- **Hairline** (ops-hairline): the default 1px border and row divider.
- **Rule Grey** (ops-rule): stronger borders for table header rules, outline buttons and dashed file drops.
- **Paper White** (ops-ink): workspace text, and also the primary button fill (the primary action is near-white on black, not green).
- **Helper Grey** (ops-muted): helper text under a value, captions and table headers. Labels people read as controls use Paper White instead.
### No-Hue Landing
The landing's colours are a separate group from the workspace tokens above. They use no hue at all.
- **Landing Black** (landing-black): the landing ground, graded down to #060606 at the foot. Black at partial alpha is the scrolled header (74%), the hero scrim and the drop shadows.
- **White Light** (landing-light): the landing's only colour, used as light at graded alphas. The reused steps are: 0.08 for the hairline, 0.1 for the timeline staff and mock-card edges, 0.13-0.16 for segment edges, the strong hairline and dashed credit outlines, 0.45 for the pill ring and the credit swatch, and 0.7-0.85 for lit edges, glow cores and signature strokes. The other alphas are stops inside glows and gradients (pillar, mark, beam). Solid white is the focus ring, the lit dots and the top of the solid pill.
- **Hairline** (landing-hairline) and **Strong Hairline** (landing-hairline-strong): the card border and divider, and the pill-ghost, state-pill and field border.
- **Silver White** (landing-ink): landing headlines and primary text, and the fill of lit day and state pills.
- **Soft Silver** (landing-soft): the fact strip and secondary values.
- **Quiet Silver** (landing-muted): landing body and ledes (7.8:1 on black).
- **Pill Ink** (landing-pill-ink): text on white pills and lit day markers.
- **Pill Silver** (landing-pill-base): the bottom stop of the solid pill's white-to-silver gradient.
- **Gloss Black** (landing-card) to **Gloss Base** (landing-card-base): the glossy card gradient.
- **Mock Card** (landing-mock-top to landing-mock-base): the raised gradient of the mock wallet and form cards in "Try it".
- **Segment Graphite** (landing-segment-top to landing-segment-base): the unsplit escrow bar in the partial-claim demonstration.

### Named Rules
**The One Accent Rule.** Green appears in the workspace only for active and success states. If a green element does not mean "you are here", "this is focused" or "this is done", it is wrong.

**The Three Meanings Rule.** Amber means waiting, red means a claim or a problem, and green means active or done. No hue is ever used for a role, a party or decoration; buyer and supplier read from an icon and a word, never a colour.

**The No-Hue Landing Rule.** The landing has no hue at all. Light carries emphasis: glows, white-gradient fills and luminous lines.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Bricolage Grotesque (via next/font, optical size pinned to 96), falling back to Mona Sans. It is used on the landing only.
**Body Font:** Mona Sans (self-hosted variable, weight 200-900, width 75-125%), falling back to system-ui.
**Label/Mono Font:** ui-monospace stack, for hashes and addresses in the workspace (`code`).

**Character:** Bricolage gives the landing's few big sentences an editorial grotesque voice. Mona Sans does all the working text in both rooms, sometimes at a slightly wider width (104-108%) for landing card titles and the wordmark.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (620, fluid up to 5.5rem, 1.04): the landing hero and close headline. Each sentence holds one line at every width, and the close never outranks the hero.
- **Headline** (600, clamp 2rem-3.5rem, 1.06): landing section heads and the contract tile title.
- **Landing lede** (400, 1-1.125rem, 1.6, max 60ch): the paragraph under a landing head, in Quiet Silver.
- **Landing card titles** (Mona Sans, 600, at 104% width): bento tile heads (clamp 1.25-1.5rem), timeline entry titles (clamp 1.0625-1.25rem), split-legend figures (clamp 1.125-1.375rem) and spec values (1.25rem). The contract tile title uses Bricolage at clamp 1.75-2.75rem.
- **Landing text steps**: 15.5px for actions and lane heads, 15px for card body, 14px for nav and meta, 13px for captions, citations and notes, and 17px for the wordmark. Mock UI inside the "Try it" illustrations uses 11px and 10.5px; those sizes stay inside the mocks.
- **Title** (700, 26px, 1.16): the workspace page and order titles (order header 28px). Normal width, opsz 36.
- **Section** (700, 15.5px): panel and card headings. List card heads are 1rem/600 and action heads 19px.
- **Metric** (650, 1.875rem, 1.1): money tile values, with the unit in a 0.875rem muted `small`. On mobile it drops to 1.375rem.
- **Body** (400, 14px, 1.5): the workspace base. Tables and forms run at 13.5px.
- **Label** (600, 12px): pills, table headers, fact labels and helper text.

### Named Rules
**The Flat Headline Rule.** Headlines are flat near-white (Silver White on the landing, Paper White in the workspace). Size and weight carry emphasis. There is no gradient text.

**The Honest Figures Rule.** Amounts use Mona Sans's default lining, proportional figures; never set `tabular-nums` on money, because it brings in the slashed zero. Money columns are right-aligned with `white-space: nowrap`. Hashes and addresses are monospace.

**The No Eyebrow Rule.** No small uppercase label ever sits above a heading. The heading starts the block.

## Layout

**Landing.** Centred content in a wrap of `min(100% - 32px, 1200px)` (64px of gutter from 720px up, and 1320px from 1500px). Sections pad `clamp(88px, 10vw, 152px)` top and bottom. The hero is at least one full viewport (min 780px) with the headline, sub, actions, glowing mark and fact strip stacked on a centre axis. The credit-versus-escrow timeline is a three-column grid (credit lane, 112px day column, escrow lane) around a centre staff, with a sticky lane header under the fixed 68px site header. Below 820px it becomes one column with the staff on the left edge. The bento runs 1, then 2 (from 720px), then 6 columns (from 1100px). Actions stack full-width below 520px.

**Workspace.** A fixed 260px sidebar (72px collapsed, and an off-canvas drawer below 767px), a 64px sticky header blurred over 80% ground, and a main column capped at 1320px with 24px padding and a 24px gap (16px on mobile). The four money tiles sit in a 4-column grid, dropping to 2 columns below 1180px. The order detail is a main column plus a 340px rail with a sticky six-step stepper bar. Below 1050px it becomes a **single column**, led by the rail's next-action panel. The orders register is a table from 760px up and **stacked rows below 760px** (reference and amount on the first line, then parties, status, next step and Open).

Spacing steps are 4, 8, 16, 20 (tile and panel padding) and 24px. Readable text is capped at 60-76ch.

## Elevation & Depth

The workspace is flat and tonally layered: ground, sidebar, card and raised grey step up in lightness at a fixed cool hue, and 1px hairlines separate them. Shadows are reserved for things that float above the page. The landing is the opposite: depth is light. Glossy cards carry an inset top highlight and a long soft drop, and lit elements glow white.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Dialog** (`box-shadow: 0 24px 60px oklch(0 0 0 / .55)`): modal dialogs (order, money, consent).
- **Menu** (`box-shadow: 0 16px 40px oklch(0 0 0 / .5)`): the user menu popover.
- **Tooltip** (`box-shadow: 0 6px 18px oklch(0 0 0 / 0.50)`): help-hint tips (near-white on black).
- **Pinned bar** (`box-shadow: 0 8px 20px oklch(0 0 0 / .35)`): the sticky stepper bar on the order page.
- **Glossy card** (`box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,.06), 0 1px 2px rgba(0,0,0,.6), 0 28px 56px -32px rgba(0,0,0,.95)`): every landing card.
- **Lit glow** (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(255,255,255,.3)`): a landing element in its "on" state (lit day pill, active lane state, split paid segment).

### Named Rules
**The Float-Only Rule.** In the workspace a shadow means "this floats": dialogs, menus, tooltips and the pinned stepper. Cards at rest have none.

**The Light Is Emphasis Rule.** On the landing, an element becomes important by lighting up (white fill plus glow), never by changing hue.

## Shapes

The workspace uses gently rounded rectangles: 8px (`--radius`) for buttons, inputs, nav rows and avatars; 12px (`--radius-lg`) for cards, tiles, panels and dialogs; 6px for pills and role tags; full rounds only for status dots, count badges and the network pill. Borders are 1px throughout; dashed borders mark something provisional (file drops, add-line buttons). The landing is softer and more luminous: 18px glossy cards, 12px inner mock cards, a 30px squircle for the mark (29px on its inner face), full pills for every action and day marker, 14px ends on the split bar, and 3px ticks on swatches and progress segments. A dashed outline on the landing means an unsecured invoice (credit); a solid silver fill means locked money (escrow).

## Components

### Buttons
Quiet and decisive in the workspace, luminous on the landing.
- **Shape:** workspace 8px radius, 40px tall (32-34px small); landing full pill, 50px tall (38px small).
- **Primary:** the workspace primary is Paper White fill with Operations Black text (13.5-14px, 600). The landing solid pill is a white-to-#e4e4e7 gradient with Pill Ink text and a soft white glow.
- **Hover / Focus:** the workspace primary mixes 14% ground into the fill on hover and 22% on press. Outline buttons move from Panel Black to Raised Grey. Focus is a 2px green outline offset by 2px. Landing pills lift 1px and brighten their glow, arrows nudge 2-3px, and a press scales to 0.975.
- **Outline / Ghost:** the workspace outline is a Rule Grey border on Panel Black. The landing ghost is a 2% white fill with a 14% white hairline, and the outline pill is a 24% white border.
- **Destructive:** a Claim Red fill; the outline danger variant is a red border and red text.

### Chips
- **Style:** a 24px-tall, 6px-radius status pill, 12px/600, with a 6px current-colour dot before the label. The fill is the soft state tint and the text is the full hue.
- **State:** green for funded, in transit and success; amber for waiting; red for claims; Raised Grey with muted or ink text for neutral. A live pill "breathes" with a 5px ring in its own hue (2.2s). Role tags share the shape and are neutral grey with an icon.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** 12px in the workspace; 18px on the landing.
- **Background:** Panel Black (quiet variants mix 60% card into the ground); landing Gloss Black gradient.
- **Shadow Strategy:** none at rest in the workspace; the glossy card shadow on the landing (see Elevation).
- **Border:** a 1px Hairline; landing 8% white.
- **Internal Padding:** 20px (16px on mobile); landing tiles clamp 22-34px.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** Raised Grey fill, 1px Hairline, 8px radius, 40px tall, 13.5px text; muted placeholder at full opacity. The label sits above in 12.5px/650 ink.
- **Focus:** the border turns green with a 3px green ring at 30%.
- **Error / Disabled:** a red border on `aria-invalid`; disabled controls drop to 45% opacity.

### Navigation
- **Sidebar:** 42px rows, 8px radius, 14px/550 muted text. Hover fills with Raised Grey at 55%. The active row is a Raised Grey fill with Paper White text, a green icon and a 4px green bar on the left edge. Count badges are near-white pills. It collapses to 72px icons and, below 767px, becomes a drawer over a 60% black backdrop.
- **Header:** the page title at 1.25rem/600, a network pill (green dot when connected, amber tint on the wrong network), the New order button and the user menu.
- **Landing header:** fixed and transparent at the top, then 74% black with a 14px blur and a hairline once scrolled. Nav links are muted full pills that brighten on hover.

### Money Tiles (signature)
Four Overview tiles in a 12px-radius Panel Black grid: a muted 14px title with an optional help hint, a 36px muted icon square, a Metric value with its unit, and a caption. They enter with a 200ms rise staggered 60ms apart; this is the workspace's only entry motion. Linked tiles lift their border to Rule Grey on hover.

### Release Plan Bar (signature)
One proportional bar split into deposit, dispatch and delivery segments with shared column templates, so each label sits over the money it describes. Paid or planned segments are solid (Paper White, then Helper Grey, then Rule Grey). Held money is Raised Grey with an inset Rule Grey edge, disputed money is the soft red with a red edge, and refunded money is muted. Container queries reveal the share, amount and state only when a segment is wide enough to read.

### Credit-versus-Escrow Timeline (landing signature)
A centre staff that fills with light as you scroll, with a glowing point of light travelling down it. Day pills light from hairline to solid white. Credit entries are dashed, right-aligned outlines; escrow entries are glossy cards that gain a white glow when lit. Entries fade under the sticky lane header instead of being sliced by it.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep green for active and success only: the active nav, focus ring, checked controls, completed steps, connected dot and success pills.
- **Do** pair every state hue with its 14% soft fill, never a solid saturated block behind text.
- **Do** make the workspace primary button near-white on black (`ops-ink` on `ops-ground`), not green.
- **Do** right-align money columns with `white-space: nowrap`, in lining proportional figures; set hashes and addresses in the monospace stack.
- **Do** use Bricolage Grotesque only for landing display and section headlines; everything else is Mona Sans.
- **Do** collapse the order detail to one column below 1050px, led by the next action, and turn the orders table into stacked rows below 760px.
- **Do** honour `prefers-reduced-motion` with final states and no travel, as both rooms already do.

### Don't:
- **Don't** introduce any hue on the landing; white light is the only colour there.
- **Don't** use colour to tell buyer from supplier; use an icon and a word.
- **Don't** set gradient text or tint headlines; headlines are flat near-white.
- **Don't** put an eyebrow or kicker label above a heading.
- **Don't** set `tabular-nums` on amounts; it brings in Mona Sans's slashed zero.
- **Don't** give workspace cards a shadow at rest; shadows are for dialogs, menus, tooltips and the pinned stepper.
- **Don't** add entry animation to workspace pages beyond the Overview tile stagger.
